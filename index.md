From 2024 to 2026, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the [Beijing International Center for Mathematical Research](https://bicmr.pku.edu.cn/) (BICMR), Peking University. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the Southern University of Science and Technology in 2024 under the supervision of Prof. Raúl Ures, and I was a visiting research scholar at Penn State University.

My research is in **dynamical systems and ergodic theory**, with a particular focus on partially hyperbolic dynamics, accessibility, ergodicity, and the topology of 3-manifolds. I am interested in how geometric and topological structures govern the long-term behavior of smooth dynamical systems.
