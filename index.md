I am currently a postdoctoral fellow at the [Beijing International Center for Mathematical Research](https://bicmr.pku.edu.cn/) (BICMR), Peking University, supervised by Prof. [Disheng Xu](https://sites.google.com/view/dishengxu/homepage), Prof. [Shaobo Gan](https://math.pku.edu.cn/jsdw/js_20180628175159671361/g_20180628175159671361/69918.htm), and Prof. [Lingrui Ge](https://sites.google.com/uci.edu/lingrui-ges-homepage/home). I received my PhD degree from the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech) in 2024 under the supervision of Prof. [Raúl Ures](https://math.sustech.edu.cn/c/uresraul). I served as a Visiting Research Scholar at Penn State University from January to May 2024 and as a Visiting Scholar at SUSTech from July to September 2024.

My research is in **dynamical systems and ergodic theory**, with a particular focus on partially hyperbolic dynamics, accessibility, ergodicity, and the topology of 3-manifolds. I am interested in how geometric and topological structures govern the long-term behavior of smooth dynamical systems.
