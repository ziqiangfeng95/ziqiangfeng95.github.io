<h2 align="center">高等数学A（一） Advanced Mathematics A (Part I), Fall 2026</h2>

This course is designed to provide students with a solid foundation in fundamental mathematical concepts, theories, and methods, while developing their ability to understand and apply mathematical ideas in a rigorous and effective manner.

Throughout the course, students are expected to understand key ideas and concepts, master essential methods and techniques, grasp the underlying principles of mathematical theories, and apply mathematical knowledge proficiently to various problems. Emphasis is placed not only on computational skills but also on a deeper understanding of the mathematical reasoning behind definitions, theorems, and methods.

Through systematic study and practice, students should gradually develop mathematical intuition, rigorous logical reasoning, and abstract thinking skills. They are also expected to strengthen their ability to formulate, analyze, and solve problems using appropriate mathematical concepts, methods, and tools. Ultimately, the course aims to cultivate a sound mathematical foundation and the analytical abilities necessary for further study in mathematics, science, engineering, and related disciplines.

### Things to keep in mind

- **Be an independent learner.** Independent learning is an important part of university education. Take responsibility for your own learning and do not rely solely on what is covered in class.
- **Read the textbook.** The textbook is an essential course resource. You are responsible for assigned readings and materials, whether or not they are discussed in class.
- **Lectures are not an exam checklist.** Lectures and exams follow the same course syllabus and learning objectives, but they do not necessarily cover exactly the same material. Some material discussed in class may not appear on an exam, and some material on an exam may not have been explicitly discussed in class.
- **Do not rely solely on lectures when preparing for exams.** When studying, pay attention to the syllabus, textbook, assigned materials, and course learning objectives rather than trying to predict an exam solely from lecture content.
- **Study consistently throughout the semester.** Regular reading, review, practice, and independent study are expected.
